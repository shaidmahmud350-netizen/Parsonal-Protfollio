06 · Framework

Amazon Product Research & Decision Framework

Every product recommendation follows a structured research methodology combining market intelligence, machine learning, financial modeling, supplier collaboration, and strategic analysis to identify profitable opportunities while minimizing business risk.

01
📊
STEP 01

Market Opportunity Analysis

Evaluate market size, monthly revenue, unit sales, and search demand to determine whether a product category has strong business potential.

Combines category-level revenue and unit-sales benchmarks with search demand signals to filter out categories that look attractive on the surface but lack durable buyer demand.

02
🔍
STEP 02

Market Intelligence

Collect historical sales data, pricing, keyword insights, BSR history, search volume, and competitor metrics using Helium 10 and Amazon research tools.

Helium 10 Keepa Amazon

Pulls together pricing history, BSR trends, and keyword-level search volume so every downstream decision is grounded in real marketplace data rather than assumptions.

03
📈
STEP 03

Machine Learning Market Growth

Use statistical analysis and machine learning models to calculate Year-over-Year market growth, identify long-term trends, and evaluate market stability.

Applies regression and trend-decomposition models to separate genuine YoY growth from seasonal noise, flagging categories with stable long-term momentum.

04
📉
STEP 04

Demand Forecasting

Forecast future demand using historical sales data and time-series analysis to estimate future market potential.

Time-series models project demand forward, giving a realistic read on future market potential before any commitment to inventory or suppliers is made.

05
🎯
STEP 05

Market Positioning

Analyze market maturity, competition level, pricing segments, and positioning opportunities to determine where a new product can succeed.

Maps pricing tiers and competitive density across the category to find the positioning gap a new entrant can realistically win.

06
🏆
STEP 06

Competitor Research

Study top competitors, pricing strategies, product listings, reviews, branding, keywords, and customer engagement to uncover competitive advantages.

Benchmarks top listings on pricing, content, and engagement to surface concrete gaps a new product and listing strategy can exploit.

07
⭐
STEP 07

Customer Pain Point Analysis

Analyze negative customer reviews to identify recurring issues and convert customer frustrations into product improvement opportunities.

Mines 1-3 star reviews across competing listings to identify recurring complaints, turning each one into a concrete product-improvement spec.

08
⚙️
STEP 08

Product Development

Collaborate with suppliers on product customization, packaging, pricing, quality improvements, MOQ negotiation, and manufacturing specifications.

Works directly with manufacturers to translate research findings into a manufacturing spec, negotiating MOQ, quality standards, and pricing along the way.

09
💰
STEP 09

Profitability Analysis

Calculate manufacturing cost, shipping, Amazon fees, advertising cost, storage cost, ROI, and projected profit before making the final decision.

Rolls up landed cost, Amazon fees, advertising spend, and storage cost into a single ROI model that has to clear a minimum margin threshold before moving forward.

10
✅
STEP 10

Final Business Decision

Combine every analysis into a final executive recommendation to determine whether the product should be launched or rejected.

Every prior step rolls up into a single go/no-go recommendation, presented to leadership with the supporting data behind it.
